Notices and cues

A notice, such as Sent or Too short, appears over whatever screen is up for about two and a half seconds. The three notices of a firmware update, Updated, Update failed, and Update undone, stay for six seconds instead, because they come once the device has worked on the update by itself, often after a restart, when you may not be looking. It is a black band across the bottom of the panel with two lines in white: what happened, and what to do about it. The band covers the screen’s bottom lines, such as the waiting count, until the notice goes. The rest of the screen stays where it is, so only the band comes and goes. Find yours below by its first line, and open it to see the whole screen, drawn over the idle screen, where nearly every notice is given.

After a sync

Section titled “After a sync”
SentEvery note in this sync reached the hub.
On the panel
SentNotes at the hub
What to do

Nothing.

Not at homeYou asked for a sync and none of your WiFi networks is in range.
On the panel
Not at homeNotes kept
What to do

Sync again when you are home.

Sync failedWiFi would not connect, the network or the hub had a problem part way, or the hub is limiting how much this device uploads.
On the panel
Sync failedNotes kept
What to do

Nothing; the notes wait for the next sync. If it keeps happening, check the WiFi passwords in config.txt, and that the network uses WPA2 or newer. After a long time away, a backlog of more than 1,000 notes can reach the hub’s limit; the notes it did not take go in later syncs.

Key refusedThe hub refused the device key.
On the panel
Key refusedCheck device_key
What to do

Check device_id and device_key in config.txt against the hub.

Hub not foundhub_url points somewhere that is not a Cairn hub.
On the panel
Hub not foundCheck hub_url
What to do

Check hub_url in config.txt.

TLS failedThe secure connection to the hub failed.
On the panel
TLS failedhub_url or ca.pem
What to do

Check hub_url, the hub’s certificate, and /cairn/ca.pem if you put one on the card; see The hub’s certificate.

Clock not setThe device reached a network but could not learn the time.
On the panel
Clock not setSync again later
What to do

Sync again later; see The clock.

After Key refused or Hub not found, the device stops syncing automatically, so it does not keep sending a key the hub will refuse. It starts again once you choose Sync from the menu, or once the device wakes with a changed config.txt. The device reads config.txt only when it wakes or the card is put back, so after you fix the file, let the device go to sleep and wake it before you sync; a Sync chosen before that tries the old settings again.

Cues

Section titled “Cues”

The board also gives a cue, a tone or a light depending on the board, when a recording starts, when it stops, when the tag changes, when a sync succeeds, when a firmware update has installed and passed its check, when something goes wrong, and when the battery is too low for a sync, a recording, or a firmware update. A firmware update that worked gives the same cue as a sync that succeeded. A cue never plays while the microphone is live, so it never ends up in a note.
