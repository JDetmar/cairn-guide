Screen flow

Every screen the panel can show, and what moves it on. Idle is home: every way leads back to it. Choose a screen to read what it tells you.

  • You press a button or change the card
  • Cairn moves on by itself
  • Taga button on the case

  1. Idle

    • Record to Recording
    • hold Tag for 0.6 seconds to Menu
    • after 20 seconds with nothing happening to Asleep

  2. Recording

    • Record again, or let go after a hold to Saving

  3. Saving

    • once the note is safe on the card to Tagging
    • when the take was too short or silent, and is thrown away to Idle

  4. Tagging

    • Tag for the next tag, and four more seconds, staying on Tagging
    • Record, keeping the tag, for a new note at once to Recording
    • when four seconds pass with no press to Syncing

  5. Syncing

    • when the sync is done to Idle
    • Record, which stops the sync at once; it runs again after the new note to Recording
    • hold Tag for 0.6 seconds, as on Idle to Menu

  6. Menu

    • after 10 seconds with no press, when the menu closes by itself to Idle
    • Sync to Syncing
    • Power off, when nothing is running to Off
    • Power off, during a sync, while new firmware checks itself, or while the device waits to restart into it to Powering off

  7. Powering off

    • once the sync has stopped, or the check is over to Off
    • instead of turning off, when it was waiting to restart into new firmware: it restarts to Idle
    • Tag, which keeps the device on, when it was stopping a sync to Syncing
    • Tag, which keeps the device on, when it was waiting for new firmware to Idle
    • Record, which keeps the device on and starts a recording at once to Recording

  8. Off

    The panel keeps this screen, and it says how to turn the device on again.

    • on battery, Record held until the device turns on to Idle
    • Tag, while the panel is still drawing this screen, or at any time plugged in, where turning off only puts the device to sleep to Idle
    • Record, while the panel is still drawing this screen, or at any time plugged in, which starts a recording at once to Recording

  9. Asleep

    The panel keeps this screen, using no power, until a press wakes the device.

    • Tag to Idle
    • Record, which wakes the device straight into a recording to Recording
    • Tag, with an update on the card to Updating
    • a press that wakes the device with no card in it, or one it cannot read to No card

  10. No card

    After a minute with no card the device goes to sleep, and the panel keeps this screen.

    • the card put back, on a press or the five-second retry to Idle
    • the card put back with an update on it to Updating

  11. Updating

    • Record, which stops the update, or records first if the update is already written to Recording
    • once the new firmware has restarted, or when the update fails to Idle
    • Tag, while the panel is still drawing this screen; the update waits for the next wake to Idle
